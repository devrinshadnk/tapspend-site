TapSpend: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

TapSpend ("the app") is made by Rinshad Nadukkandi. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Your expenses stay with you

The expenses, categories, notes and settings you enter are stored on your iPhone. They are never sent to us, and we have no server that receives them.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, TapSpend keeps your expenses in sync across your devices using Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) private database. That data lives in your own iCloud account, under Apple's terms. We cannot see or access it.

Smart features

On supported devices, features like category suggestions and spending summaries use Apple's on-device model. Your data is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us or to any AI service.

Purchases

TapSpend Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. To check whether you own Pro and to restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives your purchase history for this app and an anonymous identifier it creates. The identifier is not your name, email or Apple ID. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Exports and sharing

When you export your data (CSV, PDF or JSON) or share it, the file goes only where you choose in the iOS share sheet.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We don't sell or share personal data

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
