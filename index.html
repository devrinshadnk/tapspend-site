Common questions

I bought TapSpend Pro on another device. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase, using the same Apple ID.

How do I log with Back Tap? In the app, open Settings › Set Up Back Tap and follow the steps. Then double-tap the back of your iPhone to add an expense.

Where is my data? On your iPhone. If iCloud is on, it also syncs through your own private iCloud account.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
